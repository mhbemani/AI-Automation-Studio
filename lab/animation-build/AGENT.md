# Agent brief: "animation-build" — rigs, controls and the prebuilt animation library (phase 1: foundations)

You are a **character technical artist and animator agent**. The creator and the project lead have defined version 1 of the studio system (`docs/V1_MANIFEST.md`, git tag `v1`) and a list of about 100 animations to prebuild (`lab/animation-library/ANIMATION_LIST.md`). Your job in this task is **phase 1 of that list: the foundations**, which needs the rigs to be finished first. You work live in Blender with the creator watching, reproducibly by scripts, and you check in at every milestone. Quality matters more than speed: this library will be reused in every scene.

## Read first (in this order)
`docs/V1_MANIFEST.md`, `lab/BRIEF.md` (project rules: $0, minimal, ask before big downloads, never `pkill` anything you did not start), `lab/animation-library/ANIMATION_LIST.md` (the plan: sections 0 = rig status, 1-10 = the animations, build order, open questions), `lab/asset-lineup/AGENT.md` + `lab/results/asset-lineup.md` (what the lineup file contains), `lab/character-selection/NOTES.md` + `EMOTIONS.md` + `scripts/` (everything done on the human rig and what is left), `lab/scene-realistic-car/SCENE_NOTES.md` and `scripts/animate.py` + `_anim_util.py` (how the scene animates today: emotions, blink, speech pulse, car, phone; reuse good ideas and code), `lab/results/scene-polycount-comparison.md`, `lab/tools/polycount.py`. For the live bridge and working rules (undo steps, never kill/quit a Blender you did not start, the creator's edits are theirs) read `lab/scene-lowpoly-car/AGENT.md`; the bridge scripts are in `lab/scene-realistic-car/bridge/` (copy them).

## Creator decisions that already apply

Face deliverables: eyes, jaw/mouth, lids, brows, `Blink`, `MouthOpen`, and the 8 emotion keys (`neutral, happy, curious, excited, surprised, thoughtful, proud, amused`), readable at about 70 px face height (two-shot) and acceptable at 155 px full figure; keep the low-poly look. - **Claude gets a small armature** (no mouth: body and eye emotions only). Current Claude: `LU_V3A_CHAR_Claude` in the lineup, 11 separate parts (body root with origin on the ground, `Arm_L/R` pivot at the shoulder, `Eye_L/R` + glints, `Leg_1-4` pivot at the hip) plus cushion `LU_V3A_Cushion` with a `Squash` shape key, magic ring, and the creator's variant faces V3/V3B/V3C and poses (running, sitting, sleeping, happy standing). Keep his look exactly (the creator likes it); rig it so **parts follow bones**, add eye/blush/glint controls, squash and stretch control, and **emotion sliders as custom properties** (drivers) for eye shape/height/glint, blush, arm angle, body squash.
- **Car:** the hero coupe with doors, wheels, steering wheel, dash monitor in the lineup. Build controls: wheel spin from a speed property (driver), steering linked to the front wheels, door open/close with ease, steering sway.
- $0, no accounts, no downloads without asking, no add-ons except Blender's bundled Rigify; Opus-level care: look at every result.
- Budgets: whole human at most about 12K tris (it is about 4K now), Claude and car unchanged unless needed; Eevee, 1K textures; footprint during a draft render at most 2.5 GB (8 GB M2).

## Phase 1 scope (foundations; the "Build order" step 1 of the list, plus what it needs)
**Rigs:** (a) human rig finished enough to animate: rebind the weights to the creator's moved bones keeping hand/finger weights, thumb symmetry, close the hood opening, scale to the scene's 1.75 m (or decide and record the height), controls (IK/FK, finger masters), toes, **the face** (above); colours/materials for hoodie, pants, skin, hair, shoes using the earlier palette (hoodie `#34858d`, joggers `#515565`, skin `#e6c4ac`, hair `#5a4c41`, shoes `#f1f1ed`) assigned by region, **showing the creator 2 options before applying**; (b) Claude armature and controls; (c) car controls; (d) phone parenting/attachment point (`DEF-hand.R` equivalent) so the phone can follow the right hand.
**Animations (names from the list):** `CLA_idle`, `CLA_blink`, `CLA_emoeyes_<emotion>` and `CLA_emobody_<emotion>` (8 each), `CLA_talk_pulse` (envelope-driven via a property, plus a bakeable version), the `CLA_look_*` set; `HUM_seat_idle`, `HUM_blink`, `HUM_emoface_<emotion>` (8) and `HUM_emobody_<emotion>` (8), `HUM_talk_small`, `HUM_talk_nod`, `HUM_look_at_*`; `CAR_wheel_spin`, `CAR_steering_sway`, `CAR_steering_turn_<dir>`, `CAR_door_open/close_L/R`. Everything 24 fps, named exactly as in the list, loops tile perfectly, durations as listed (note deviations).
Phases 2-4 of the list (story scenes, signature moments, extras) are **not** part of this task: stop after phase 1 and report.

## Quality bars and how to prove them
- **Rig tests:** a stress-pose contact sheet for the human (arms raised, elbows 120, head turn +-70, spine twist, seated, crouch, ankle flex, finger curls) and for Claude (squash/stretch extremes, arms, eye extremes), with a numeric check that nothing explodes or intersects badly; poke-through for the clothed regions.
- **Animation checks (numeric):** every loop's first and last pose match (max difference), frame counts match the list, no bone leaves its range, no foot sliding where relevant, eye/blink timing sane, `CLA_talk_pulse` follows a test envelope within one frame.
- **Watchable previews:** since stills cannot show motion, render a **preview reel per group** as small low-quality videos (Eevee draft 540p, 24 fps, every action labelled) into `lab/animation-build/previews/` and tell the creator which files to watch; you cannot see motion in real time yourself: say so and judge from consecutive-frame strips and the numbers, and ask the creator for their verdict on feel.
- **Emotion readability:** contact sheets of all 8 emotions for Claude (eyes + body) and the human (face + body) at the two target sizes, with an honest note of which ones read poorly; iterate at least twice on the weak ones.
- **Contract test:** copy `lab/scene-realistic-car/scene.blend` (read-only source) to your folder and show how a scene would use the library (append the rigs and actions, play an emotion, a blink and a talk pulse on a test envelope) without editing the scene's folder; list the changes `animate.py` would need. Integrating into the scene itself is a later task.
- Measure: triangles per part (`polycount.py`), memory and time of a draft render with the rigs, library file size.
