## Task 2: an endless, closed, squarish road
Why: the car currently reaches the end of the street after about 6-7 seconds of driving, and dialogue scenes will run for 60 s or more. The road must be a **closed loop**, so the car can circle forever and the last section connects back to the first.
Design (confirm numbers by testing, adjust if something looks wrong):
- A **rounded rectangle**, about 360 m (X) by 220 m (Y) along the road centreline, with **long straights** and four corners with a radius of about 14-18 m (roads use the existing Kenney road tiles/curves; reuse `CITY_Road_*`/`CITY_Road_Curve` assets; real-scale lanes as now, 8 m wide). Perimeter about 1,100 m: at 8-9 m/s (about 30 km/h) one lap is about 2 minutes, a turn every 30-45 s. Straight sections must be long enough for ~30 s of straight driving.
- **Pickup point:** keep the exit building (door near x=1.5, y=-8) and the car's pickup curb on a **long straight**, far from corners, like now. Keep the opening shot (`CAM_EST_Street`) working: human at the door, car arrives from the left/behind.
- **Buildings and props along both sides of the whole ring** (inner block and outer side): use the existing building models, instanced (linked duplicates or collection instances; no unique copies), varied with a **fixed random seed** (reproducible), including corner buildings, trees and parked cars sparingly, street lights every ~16 m, windows lit as now. Keep visual consistency with the current look (golden hour, same palette). No visible gaps or seams, including at the loop closure; no roads ending in the void; a distant skyline/ground plane so the horizon never shows emptiness. Inside the ring: simple blocks/park/low buildings; outside: a ring of background buildings.
- **Drive path:** `CITY_DrivePath` becomes a **closed (cyclic) curve** following the right-hand lane around the whole ring with smooth corners (no abrupt heading changes). The car must be able to follow it with a Follow Path constraint at constant speed (forward axis as documented in the notes).
